Tourist Visa

This visa is issued to individuals who wish to visit the State of Kuwait for tourism and entertainment.

Visa Instructions

Tourism Visa

  • Passport validity: No less than 6 months
  • Entry permit validity: 30 days from issuance
  • Stay duration: Subject to the issued permit
  • Number of entries: Single entry
  • The visa holder is prohibited from working

Family Visit Visa

  • Passport validity: No less than 6 months
  • Entry permit validity: Subject to approval
  • Stay duration: Subject to the issued permit
  • Number of entries: Single entry
  • The visa holder is prohibited from working

Government Visa

  • Passport validity: No less than 6 months
  • Entry permit validity: Subject to approval
  • Stay duration: Subject to the issued permit
  • Number of entries: Single entry
  • Follow the conditions of the issued permit

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Kuwait visa?

A visa is an authorization for a foreign visitor to enter Kuwait, subject to the applicable immigration requirements.

How can I apply for a visa?

You can review the requirements and application procedures through the official Kuwait government website.

How can I check my application?

Use the appropriate official government service to check your application status.

Can I work with a tourist visa?

A tourist visa does authorize employment. Check the applicable official visa conditions.
